Rider Agreement & Terms

1. Eligibility & Driver Verification

The user must possess a valid Indian Driving Licence and verify their profile safely via the ZiGo Terminal.

2. Vehicle Operations & Boundaries

Riders agree to operate the vehicle responsibly and strictly within the designated municipal operational boundaries of Zirakpur.

3. Smart Hub Parking Compliance

Vehicles must be safely checked in and parked exclusively at designated Smart Mobility Hub locations (VIP Road, High Ground, or Singhpura Hubs).

4. ⏱ Grace Period & Package Rollover

Dropping off after your package timeline automatically upgrades your booking to the next applicable package base fare. Extra KMs are strictly charged at ₹6/KM.

5. ⚠️ Overtime Surcharge

A flat fee of ₹50/Hour kicks in for delays exceeding 2 hours, in addition to the standard ₹6/KM extra distance rate.
